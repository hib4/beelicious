# Storylines — Beelicious product demo

**Picked: A · Satu Pesanan.** It is the only storyline that shows both sides of the product (the student's chat and the merchant's dashboard) as one system, with one order as the carrier.

## A — Satu Pesanan (built)
- **Device and form:** one object across apps, as an object-to-object chain that goes from split screen to one.
- **The film:** one order is born in the student's WhatsApp chat: tenant, menu, quantity, cart, pickup slot. It becomes the Rp60.000 total and then the QRIS. It drops out of the chat and lands as a row on the merchant's Pesanan table. The merchant accepts it, starts preparing it and marks it ready, and each click sends its notification back into the chat. The film ends at 12.30, when the order is collected.

## B — 12.15
- **Device and form:** a moment in someone's day, cut on the clock.
- **The film:** giant timestamps carry a student's class break. They order between lectures, and the merchant's board ticks along in the background. The film ends exactly at the pickup time, when the queue is skipped.

## C — Bongkar
- **Device and form:** the object is the explanation, filmed as one continuous take.
- **The film:** the camera flies through an exploded stack of the conversation. Each reply peels away to reveal the machinery under it: the catalog, the cart, the slot capacity, the payment check and the merchant table.
