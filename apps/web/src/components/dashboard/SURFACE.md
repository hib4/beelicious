# Tenant operations

Mode: Operate. Extend the existing DESIGN.md; keep marketing composition and GSAP outside this layout.

The primary task is moving a verified paid order through acceptance, preparation, readiness and collection. Real backend data, payment/fulfillment separation, immutable item snapshots and capacity protection take precedence over decorative expression.

Use parchment/white flat surfaces, charcoal labels, Bee Yellow primary actions, Plus Jakarta Sans 24–28px page headings and Inter 14–16px operational text. Scope styles to `.dashboard`. Retain semantic brand tokens and 20px panels; use tabular, right-aligned money. Keep current account/tenant visible and distinguish simulations from live operations.

Desktop uses a 240px sidebar. At tablet widths navigation folds above content; mobile orders become complete stacked rows rather than a horizontally scrolling table. All controls remain at least 44px. Native dialogs provide focus containment and Escape behavior. No invented growth charts, totals, merchant details or customer routing identifiers.

Lists expose filters, pagination and manual refresh. Polling pauses in hidden tabs. Mutations report committed outcomes separately from WhatsApp delivery. Stale/conflicting edits require refresh. Empty/error/loading states retain the surrounding frame.

Browser validation covers 320, 390, 768 and 1440px with mocked authentication and real suite-owned PostgreSQL data. Capture screenshots and inspect them; automated accessibility is not a screen-reader certification.
