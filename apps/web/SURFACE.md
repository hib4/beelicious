# Beelicious editorial marketing direction

## Confirmed direction

Big Type. Bold Food. Playful Bee. A bold editorial poster system with the original Bee-bot, existing brand palette/Plus Jakarta Sans/Inter, licensed editorial food photographs, and an immersive numeric WhatsApp journey. Audience remains invited pilot users; ordering defaults to Segera Hadir. No production collection or public launch is claimed.

## Argument and composition

- A chat can start the journey: fullscreen LAPAR? / CHAT AJA. and the unchanged mascot on one shared charcoal canvas.
- The mechanism is simple: a short CHAT. PILIH. PICK UP. transition, tied to native scrolling.
- Food is the subject: one dominant nasi goreng photograph and two supporting food/drink crops, explicitly editorial.
- The product proves its promise: six chapters demonstrate greeting, tenant, menu/quantity, cart, pickup, and gateway instructions with realistic numeric messages.
- The pilot is concrete: Warung Babe is a large typography-led spotlight, with no invented merchant photo or availability.
- The platform is distinct from its tenants: a short Bukan restoran. manifesto.
- The story closes with the original mascot and one WhatsApp action, followed by a large Beelicious wordmark and real links/credits.

## Research direction

The design-taste-frontend draw offered Name as Monument, Loud and Flat, and Colour Chapters. Loud and Flat was selected for its short blunt statements, large type/colour mass, and appetite-led photographic composition. Direction seed: 1438792661. Palette and type were kept. The randomized square-image hero, word-tile gallery, team grid, editable headline, and two-action close were not used: they conflict with the user's explicit full-scene hero, photographic food section, merchant truth, numeric story, and single CTA. Unrelated landscape atmosphere images were rejected. The user's brief is authoritative.

## Implementation contract

The hero is readable immediately, with no preloader or text hiding. Shared operational tokens remain available; marketing has separate fluid display scales. Text/CTA readability and mascot geometry outrank overlap. Each major section has a distinct composition, equal outer gutters, and no invented commercial proof.

ExperienceMotion uses GSAP/ScrollTrigger and media contexts. One visual preview pins only at min-width1024/min-height720/fine pointer/no reduced motion. Normal-flow chapters define its duration. Complete server-rendered inline transcripts are the fallback; the decorative pinned copy is aria-hidden. Chapter links are ordinary anchors. No wheel/touch interception. Animation does not place orders or verify payments.

## Assets

No video is added: the story's moving image treatment uses existing photography and the original mascot instead of unrelated footage.
