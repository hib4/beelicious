# Storylines — Beelicious loop film

**Picked: B · Balas Angka.** It turns the product's real mechanic (you reply with a number) into the film's beat, and the product's own "0. Kembali" command is the loop seam.

## B — Balas Angka (built)
Type carries the argument · cut on the beat. Each numeric reply the student types slams in as a monumental numeral on a downbeat and flips the full-bleed field. After the hook, "Cukup WhatsApp. Tanpa aplikasi baru." sets the channel. Next to each numeral lands the bot line it triggers: tenant (Bakmi Effata) → category → dish → portions → cart → pickup slot → total → QRIS → verified → ready for pickup. The claim says "Pesan duluan. Ambil pas jamnya." The end card is the bee-bot with TINGGAL CHAT!. The last line, "0 Kembali", sends the camera through the 0 back to frame 0.

## A — Antre Itu Kemarin
Life before and after, as a chain of objects. A parchment wall of queue tickets crawls; one yellow "Halo" bubble slices it in half. Each reply becomes the next object (tenant → menu card → portions → QRIS → pickup chip). The chip then becomes the ticket on top of the re-formed wall, closing the loop.

## C — Satu Tarikan
A character leads one continuous take. The bee-bot swings in, and the camera follows its gaze down a colossal transcript tower built from chat bubbles. It then pulls back to show that the blocks spell TINGGAL CHAT!, and the bee-bot swings in again.
